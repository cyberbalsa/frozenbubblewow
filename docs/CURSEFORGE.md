# Frozen Bubble Forever

Aim, bounce shots off the walls, and match three bubbles of the same color to clear them. Drop a whole cluster by breaking the bubbles holding it up, and clear the board before the ceiling comes down.

Frozen Bubble Forever brings the original 100 levels, artwork, and music to WoW Forever. Play the campaign, chase a highscore in endless mode, challenge another player, or host an arena for up to five. In versus, the clusters you drop send extra bubbles to your opponent.

Join the **FrozenBubbleForever** in-game channel to find players, share scores, and browse player-created levels. The level editor lets you paint a board, test it, and offer it to other players for free. Downloads go directly from one player to another, so the creator needs to be online.

Everyone playing needs the addon. Blizzard controls channel visibility and addon-message delivery; the channel may not reach everyone on the same ruleset. If automatic discovery is blocked, the addon provides click-to-announce buttons and direct challenges by player name. Shared highscores are player-reported and intended for friendly competition.

Type **/fb** to play. Aim with the mouse and click to shoot, or hover over the board and use Left/Right and Space. Press P to pause solo play. Versus matches keep running when the window is hidden.

Version 0.1.0 supports WoW Forever 1.60.1. Use **/fb debug** to inspect recent diagnostics and report problems on [GitHub](https://github.com/cyberbalsa/frozenbubblewow/issues).

Frozen Bubble Forever is an unofficial, open-source port of Frozen Bubble, released under GPLv2. Original game by Guillaume Cottenceau and the Frozen-Bubble Team, artwork by Alexis Younes and Amaury Amblard-Ladurantie, and music by Matthias Le Bidan.
